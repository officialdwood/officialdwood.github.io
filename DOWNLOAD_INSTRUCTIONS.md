## After Downloading

Once you have the `wp-employee-checkin-n-out-updated.zip` file:

1. **DO NOT unzip it yet!**
2. Go to your WordPress Admin panel
3. Navigate to **Plugins** → **Add New**
4. Click **Upload Plugin**
5. Choose the `wp-employee-checkin-n-out-updated.zip` file
6. Click **Install Now**
7. Click **Activate Plugin**

That's it! The plugin is now installed and ready to use.
