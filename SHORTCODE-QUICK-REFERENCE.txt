================================================================================
WYOHOOPS GAME DATABASE - SHORTCODE
================================================================================

SHORTCODE: [wyohoops_gamedb]

================================================================================
USAGE
================================================================================

To display the WyoHoops Game Database on any WordPress page or post, simply add:

[wyohoops_gamedb]

That's it! The shortcode will render the full database interface with:
- Teams tab (with rankings, records, statistics)
- Schedule tab (past and upcoming games)
- Compare tab (team-vs-team comparison)

================================================================================
OPTIONAL PARAMETERS
================================================================================

[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="teams"]
[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="schedule"]
[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="compare"]

[wyohoops_gamedb classification="4A"]
[wyohoops_gamedb classification="3A"]
[wyohoops_gamedb classification="2A"]
[wyohoops_gamedb classification="1A"]

[wyohoops_gamedb gender="B"]  (Boys)
[wyohoops_gamedb gender="G"]  (Girls)

================================================================================
EXAMPLES
================================================================================

Show 4A teams by default:
[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="teams" classification="4A"]

Show boys schedule:
[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="schedule" gender="B"]

Show comparison tool:
[wyohoops_gamedb default_tab="compare"]

================================================================================
