Features Demonstrated

  • Square Color Chips: All color tiles are perfectly square with sharp edges (no rounded corners)
  • Hover Effect: Move your mouse over any color tile to see it enlarge smoothly
  • Click to Expand: Click any tile to open it in a full modal view
  • Close Modal: Click the X button or press ESC to close the expanded view
  • Color Names: Each color displays its name centered below in small font
  • Grid Layout: Default 6 columns per row (configurable in WordPress admin)
  • Responsive: Automatically adjusts columns on smaller screens
